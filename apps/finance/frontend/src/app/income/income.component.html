Stock bonuses and equity grants

Add stock grant

Grant shares use their grant value as compensation in the grant year, with later years counting only gains or losses. ESPP shares are employee-funded: compensation is their economic value less your purchase contributions. Sales keep their recorded cash proceeds fixed. These amounts stay out of recurring pay and net worth.

For listed employer stock, enter its ticker to refresh a market quote when this page opens and after you save. Only the ticker is sent to the price provider. Leave it blank for private stock and enter dated prices manually.
