Encryption demo

Two participants in your browser exchange keys, encrypt a message, and verify its decryption.

How the demo works

  1. Two independent key pairs derive a shared key using ECDH and HKDF.
  2. The sender encrypts your message with AES-GCM and a fresh random initialization vector.
  3. The receiver decrypts it and verifies the authentication tag.

This demonstration runs locally. Your message and keys stay in this browser.
