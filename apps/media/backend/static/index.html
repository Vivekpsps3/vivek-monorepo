Ratings, reviews, and progress stay in this journal. Search can ask TMDB, MusicBrainz, the Cover Art Archive, Open Library, or RAWG for public titles and artwork. Optional scrape mode also asks TVMaze and the iTunes Search API, which do not need a key. The calendar refreshes episode dates for series already saved from TVMaze or TMDB, and sends a watched movie's title and year to Wikidata for sequels and spin-offs. With a TMDB key, that movie's collection is used too. Those results are not added to your library. Listening and watching history is not collected from those services.
