# Personal workspace and Town revamp

Selected direction and detailed issue plan, 2026-11-17. **Planning only:** no app
feature, production change, purchase, merge or deployment is performed by this work.

## Settled owner choices

- A personal tool and creative world, heavily stylized and gamified throughout.
- Direct selection of buildings and objects with a layered 3D yet 2D feel; no avatar
  movement. The design uses a 2.5D isometric coastal diorama and readable themed UI.
- All discussed game systems: decorating, earned furniture, residents/relationships,
  quests, exploration, minigames, progression, collectibles and a cosmetic currency.
- Full workout sessions/routines **and** personal records/progress in Gym.
- A compact practical Hub is the default arrival. Town and every useful app task
  remain directly reachable on desktop and phone with equal attention.
- Obsidian remains the knowledge/notes authority; Immich remains the photo authority.
  Pixels connects dated memories. Standalone World presentation is absorbed into
  Town/Pixels while saved concepts and supported links/data are preserved.
