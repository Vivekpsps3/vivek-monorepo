## Delivery and release boundaries

This amendment publishes planning and keeps repository/GitHub packets synchronized.
It does not implement application features. Individual future PRs ship complete
small leaves after required local checks. Integrated leaves audit real earlier
evidence, measurements, privacy/recovery and preserving rollback; they do not hide
known feature defects behind a final cleanup task.

No scope parent closes until all original and expanded outcomes have merged proof.
Owner art review, actual connector activation, off-host restore/alert proof and
production deployment remain separate. Existing user World edits are left intact.
